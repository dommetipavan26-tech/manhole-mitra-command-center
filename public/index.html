PROJECT SUMMARY

One node. Two protective functions.

Manhole-Mitra detects a missing or tilted cover, then monitors the water level inside the manhole chamber. Local LED/buzzer output is immediate; Blynk provides the remote event and status layer.

Reed + TiltAJ-SR04M + FloatESP32 + Blynk

MEASUREMENT MODEL

Internal water depth = probe-to-base height − probe-to-water gap

Internal water level is an early hydraulic-risk indicator. Reed and tilt sensors remain the direct confirmation of cover displacement.
